# SudNotch

A free notch app for macOS 14 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel), made by [Crazy Sud](https://crazysud.com/sudnotch/).

Hover over or click the MacBook notch and it opens: now playing, volume and brightness pop-ups, battery and AirPods battery, a file tray with AirDrop, clipboard history, a quick note, a timer, your next calendar events, the weather and a camera mirror. Macs without a notch and external screens get a small pill at the top centre.

No account, no tracking. Nothing leaves your Mac except the weather lookup (Open-Meteo).

## Install

1. Download **SudNotch.dmg** from the [latest release](https://github.com/sudeep-sachdeva/SudNotch/releases/latest) and drag SudNotch to Applications.
2. Open it. macOS will say it can't check the app (it isn't from the App Store or a paid developer account). Click **Done**.
3. Go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to SudNotch, then confirm.

SudNotch lives in the menu bar (no Dock icon). Use its menu bar icon for Settings and Quit.
